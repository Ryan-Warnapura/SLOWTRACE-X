# Image credits

Every image in this folder is real mission imagery. NASA imagery is generally
not copyrighted; each credit below is shown next to the image in the app.

| File | What it shows | Credit | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `spherex-allsky.webp` | SPHEREx's first all-sky map (PIA26600) | NASA/JPL-Caltech | https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/images/pia26600-spherexs-first-all-sky-map/ |
| `spherex-observatory.webp` | SPHEREx after environmental testing, November 2024 (PIA26536) | NASA/JPL-Caltech/BAE Systems | https://images.nasa.gov/details/PIA26536 |
| `spherex-first-light.webp` | First images from SPHEREx, six detectors, 27 March 2025 (PIA26280) | NASA/JPL-Caltech | https://images.nasa.gov/details/PIA26280 |
| `spherex-filters.webp` | SPHEREx detector filters (PIA25788) | NASA/JPL-Caltech | https://images.nasa.gov/details/PIA25788 |
| `spherex-vela.webp` | Vela Molecular Ridge, first public data release, July 2025 (PIA26354) | NASA/JPL-Caltech | https://images.nasa.gov/details/PIA26354 |
| `targets/*.webp` | SPHEREx Level 2 cutouts of the five challenge fields, two dates each | NASA/JPL-Caltech/IPAC (IRSA) | Rendered by `scripts/render_target_plates.py` from the recorded exposures in `public/starter.json` and `public/examples/` |

The photographs were downloaded from the NASA Image and Video Library, resized
and converted to WebP. No image is hotlinked, generated or stock.

The sky layers in `public/atlas/` are credited on the Sky Atlas page and in the
README.
